Personal project · 2026

Health app (Swift)

RESET is a native iOS recovery companion I am building in Swift (SwiftUI, SwiftData, HealthKit). It reads sleep, resting heart rate and HRV from Apple Health to produce a daily recovery score, then suggests that day's training and meals. A care tab keeps appointments in one place, and a small pixel-art mascot reflects how recovery is going. Work in progress, code not public yet.

Portfolio PDF ↗EUCI portfolios ↗
