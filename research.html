The Health Consequences of Workers in Healthcare Systems Managing Cov-Sars-19 Outbreaks in Spain

GitHub ↗

A quantitative analysis using primary survey data from the MINDCOVID project (IMIM, Barcelona). Examined anxiety, PTSD, panic attacks, and suicidal ideation among 9,138 healthcare professionals across 19 Spanish hospitals during the initial pandemic wave (May 2020). Findings show elevated anxiety, signs of early PTSD and suicidal ideation among participants. For the figures, read the full study on GitHub or ask me.

9,138healthcare workers19Spanish hospitalsMay 2020first pandemic wave

Methods: STATA · Multilevel regression · Multivariate analysis · GAD-7 · PTSD-4
Universitat Pompeu Fabra — Masters in Research in Sociology and Demography · Supervisor: Prof. Pau Baizan, ICREA Research Professor, UPF

Data source: Este estudio se ha realizado utilizando los datos anonimizados cedidos por la Fundació Institut Hospital del Mar d'Investigacions Mèdiques en el marco del Estudio Mind Covid ISCIII FEDER COV20/00711.
